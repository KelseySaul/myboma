# Design System: MyBoma (Eduka Clean Fintech Edition)

## Creative North Star: "Eduka Clean OS" (Ref: Pinterest 1128714725386615754)

A vibrant, friendly, high-contrast dashboard system featuring:
- **Canvas Ground:** Soft clean off-white `#f4f6fa`
- **Card Containers:** Pure white `#ffffff` with `rounded-3xl` (24px) corners and soft borders.
- **Top 4 Vibrant Solid Cards:**
  - **Green Card (`bg-[#00c569]`):** Total Units / Portfolio Capacity
  - **Blue Card (`bg-[#0094ff]`):** Active Tenants / Leases
  - **Purple Card (`bg-[#8c45ff]`):** Total Rent Collections / Revenue
  - **Orange Card (`bg-[#ff5722]`):** Subscription Plan & Node Expiry
- **Active Navigation Indicator:** Solid green left pill (`bg-[#00c569]`) with green active text and icon.
- **Charts:** Vibrant green spline collection chart + multicolored donut breakdown ring.
- **Header:** Friendly greeting ("Good Morning, [Name]! 👋") + rounded pill search bar.
