# MyBoma Privacy Policy

Effective: November 24, 2026
Version: 2026-11-24

## 1. Who we are

MyBoma is a property management and rental listing platform operated from Kenya and available at https://myboma.kelseysaul.dev. For the personal data described in this policy, MyBoma is the data controller under the Data Protection Act 2019. Where a landlord or agency uses MyBoma to manage their own tenants, that landlord or agency independently decides why and how it uses that tenant data and is responsible for that use. Contact us about privacy at support@myboma.com.

## 2. What we collect

Account details: name, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a secure hash) and your role. Landlord details: payout method and M-Pesa number or bank account details used to receive rent. Property data: listings, units, photos, rent amounts and leases. Tenant data: lease dates, invoices, payments, balances and repair requests. Payment data: amounts, dates and provider references from M-Pesa, Pesapal or Stripe; we never see your card number or M-Pesa PIN. Enquiries: the name, email address, phone number and message a house hunter sends about a listing, with or without an account. Technical data: device and browser information, IP address, and error reports used to keep the service secure and working.

## 3. Why we use it and our lawful basis

We process personal data only where the Data Protection Act 2019 allows it. To provide the service you asked for, such as your account, rent invoices, payments and enquiries, we rely on performance of a contract or steps you asked us to take before one. To keep payment, invoice and tax records, we rely on legal obligation. To secure the platform, prevent fraud and fix errors, we rely on legitimate interest. For optional messages such as product news, and for push notifications, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

## 4. Who we share it with

Landlords and agents see the details of their own tenants and of house hunters who enquire about their listings. Tenants see their landlord's name and payment details needed to pay rent. We also use service providers who process data on our behalf under contract: Neon (database hosting), Cloudflare R2 (file and photo storage), Render (application hosting), Resend (email delivery), OneSignal (push notifications), Sentry (error monitoring), Safaricom M-Pesa, Pesapal and Stripe (payments), and Google (if you choose to sign in with Google). We may disclose data where Kenyan law requires it, for example to KRA or a court, or to protect users from fraud or harm.

## 5. Transfers outside Kenya

Some of our providers store or process data outside Kenya, mainly in the United States and the European Union. We only transfer data where the Data Protection Act 2019 and the Data Protection (General) Regulations 2021 allow it, using providers that offer appropriate security safeguards and contractual protections, and we keep a record of these transfers.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep account and property data while your account is active. If you close your account, we delete or anonymise personal data that we no longer need, except where we have a lawful reason to retain it. Payment, invoice and receipt records are generally kept for at least 5 years to meet Kenyan tax and accounting requirements. When you submit a guest enquiry, we use its details to deliver the emails; the landlord, agent and email recipient may retain their copy under their own policies. We review error reports and security logs periodically and retain them only as long as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting and legal obligations.

## 7. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed. Access to each landlord's data is restricted at the database level so one landlord cannot see another's tenants. Only authorised staff can access production systems. If a personal data breach is likely to put you at real risk of harm, we will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and tell affected users without undue delay.

## 8. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act 2019 you have the right to be told how your data is used; to access your data; to correct inaccurate data; to have false, misleading or unnecessary data deleted; to restrict or object to processing; to withdraw consent; and to receive your data in a portable format. To use any of these rights, email support@myboma.com. We may need to verify your identity. Under the Data Protection (General) Regulations 2021, access requests are handled within 7 days; rectification, erasure, restriction and objections within 14 days; and portability within 30 days, subject to the applicable rules and any lawful exception. Some requests may be limited where we must keep records by law, for example payment records.

## 9. Children

MyBoma is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and protect your account. We do not use advertising cookies. Push notifications are only sent if you allow them on your device.

## 11. Emails

Service emails, such as invoices, payment receipts, rent reminders and enquiry confirmations, are part of using MyBoma. Product news is only sent if you agreed to receive it, and every such email includes an unsubscribe link.

## 12. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your data, contact support@myboma.com first. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), Kenya, at https://www.odpc.go.ke.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant, we will tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
